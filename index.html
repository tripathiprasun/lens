Recognized signatures

JPEG · PNG · GIF · WebP · PDF · ZIP · RAR · 7z · GZIP · MP3 · MP4 · WebM · WAV · EXE/PE · ELF

Other files are still hashed, entropy-scored, and hex-dumped — LENS just won't be able to name the format.

Files are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded.

LENS is a local inspection tool, not a malware scanner or a complete forensic suite. Nothing you open here ever leaves this browser tab.
